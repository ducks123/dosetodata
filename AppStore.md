## Description (App Store — 4,000 char max)

Most people can't remember how they felt three weeks ago. DoseToData fixes that.

Whether you're starting a new medication, adjusting a dose, or trying to understand why some days feel harder than others — DoseToData helps you capture what's actually happening, so you can see real patterns over time.

**Daily check-in, built for meds**
A quick daily log captures how you're feeling across the things that matter most: anxiety, mood, focus, ease to start, irritability, and more. Takes under a minute. Over time, your answers become a data set that tells a story your memory never could.

**Know if your meds are working**
DoseToData links your daily scores to your medication schedule. You'll see whether the days you took your medication trend differently than the days you didn't. Not an assumption — actual data from your own life.

**Run a test, get an answer**
Starting a new medication? Changing a dose? Create a Test and DoseToData tracks that window as its own data set. When it's over, compare it directly to your baseline. Was the new dose better? By how much? Now you know.

**Your schedule, your way**
Add the medications you take, set the times and days that match your actual routine, and get reminders when it's time. Tap "Took it" right from the lock screen — no need to open the app.

**Insights that actually mean something**
Clean charts show your trends week by week, month by month, or across a full year. A single overall score gives you the big picture at a glance, while per-question graphs let you drill into exactly what's shifting.

**Private by design**
Your data lives on your device. Sign in optionally to back up and sync across devices — but the app works fully offline and always will.

DoseToData is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always work with your doctor when making changes to your medications.
