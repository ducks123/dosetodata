A calmer way to notice what is changing.

DoseToData turns everyday observations into a timeline you can actually use: what you took, how you felt, and what shifted over days or weeks.

Daily check-ins built around meds

Log mood, anxiety, focus, irritability, energy, side effects, and custom questions without turning tracking into another chore.

Medication schedules with context

Add medication names, doses, timing, and reminders, then see how your schedule lines up with check-ins and adherence.

Tests for dose changes and switches

Create a test window when something changes, then compare that period against your baseline with simple trend views.

Bring patterns, not guesswork.

Medication changes often unfold slowly. DoseToData helps you keep track of what happened across the weeks your memory tends to blur together.
