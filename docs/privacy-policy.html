Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

DoseToData ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DoseToData mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Health & Wellness Data: DoseToData stores the health information you choose to log, including daily check-in responses (such as mood, energy, focus, and custom questions), medication names, doses, start dates, side effects, and adherence records. This data is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers.

Account Information: Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in, we collect your email address through our authentication provider (Supabase) to manage your account. Signing in does not upload your health data.

Subscription Information: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect your Apple App Store receipt data and a pseudonymous device identifier to verify and manage your subscription status. We do not receive your payment card details.

Anonymous Usage Analytics: We collect anonymous, event-level usage data — for example, which screens are viewed, whether onboarding was completed, and how often features are used. These events never include your health content: no medication names, doses, mood scores, side effects, or notes. They are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Ad Attribution: If you install the app after tapping an Apple Search Ads advertisement, Apple's AdServices framework provides an anonymous attribution token that helps us measure ad performance. This token does not identify you and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Device Information: We may collect basic device type and OS version information for compatibility and crash reporting purposes.

Notification Tokens: If you enable reminders, we store a device token to deliver local notifications for check-in and medication reminders. These tokens are not shared with third parties for marketing.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To store and display your health tracking data within the app
  • To manage your optional account sign-in
  • To manage your subscription and verify access to premium features
  • To send medication and check-in reminders (if enabled)
  • To provide trends, insights, and adherence summaries
  • To understand which features are used and improve the app, using anonymous usage analytics
  • To measure the performance of our advertising, using anonymous attribution data
  • To respond to your support requests and feedback
  • To detect and fix bugs and technical issues

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or your health data. We do not share your data with advertisers. Your data may be processed by the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: If you sign in, your email address is processed by Supabase, our authentication provider. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status, App Store receipt data, and anonymous Apple Search Ads attribution tokens are processed by RevenueCat. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • PostHog: Anonymous usage events (never health content) are processed by PostHog, our analytics provider. See posthog.com/privacy.

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

4. Health Data

DoseToData is designed to keep your health data private. All health and medication data you enter is stored on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers and never shared with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes. Our anonymous usage analytics contain no health content of any kind.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

  • View and delete your tracking data at any time within the app
  • Disable notifications at any time through your device settings
  • Cancel your subscription at any time through the App Store
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us

7. Data Retention

Your health data lives on your device and persists until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app. To request permanent deletion of any associated data, contact us and we will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

DoseToData is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
