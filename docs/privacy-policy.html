Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2025

DoseToData ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DoseToData mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account via Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address.

Health & Wellness Data: We collect the information you voluntarily enter, including medications, doses, schedules, daily check-in responses (such as mood, focus, anxiety, and custom questions), medication adherence logs, and test period notes.

Device Information: We collect basic device and OS information for compatibility and crash reporting purposes.

Notification Tokens: If you enable reminders, we store a device token to deliver local notifications. These are scheduled on your device and never transmitted to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide your check-in history, streaks, and wellness trends
  • To display medication adherence and test period insights
  • To send local notifications for check-in and medication reminders
  • To respond to your support requests and feedback
  • To detect and fix bugs and technical issues

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers. Your data may be processed by the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Cloud database and authentication provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple Sign In: Used for account authentication. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit and encryption at rest. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Your Rights

  • Request access to or correction of your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Control notification preferences on your device

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at stewartsherpa1@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. To delete your account and all associated data, email us and we will process your request within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

DoseToData is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at stewartsherpa1@gmail.com.
